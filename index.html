How to use Plotcraft

1
Pick a series. Click a chip in the toolbar above the chart to make it active — that's the one you're drawing.
2
Click the sheet to add points. Each click drops a point on the active series. Only the active series can be edited on the chart — others stay locked so you don't bump them by accident.
3
Drag a point to move it, double-click to delete it. Or edit the exact x/y numbers in the points table on the right, under "Active series".
4
Made a mistake? Hit the ↶ undo button — it steps back through adds, drags, edits and deletes, one at a time.
5
Add more series with "+ Add series". Each one gets its own color and marker shape automatically. Remove one with the × on its chip.
6
Style it on the right. Set the title, axis names, axis min/max/tick spacing, font sizes, and legend position in the panels on the right. Open "Active series" to change a series' color, marker, line style, or curve.
7
Download your figure as SVG (for papers), PNG (for slides/docs), or CSV (your raw points) from the Download button above the chart.
